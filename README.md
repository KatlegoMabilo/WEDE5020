# WEDE5020
Web development project
## Project Title
Kat Claws Spa 
## overview
 The young girl saw and took the opportunity of starting her own nail salon when she realised her love for art and skill of doing nails. 
The talented and passion driven individual aims at making other females feel more confident and express themselves easier with their bright and cheerful nails. 
## website goals and objectives
The website aims at allowing clients to book appoints more effectively and efficiently. 
The website will also display prices of each nail design. 
This website will also be user friendly with easy navigation and easier language. 
Kat Claws website will also provide an enquiry section for users.
## current website features and functionality
Kat Claws website has a detailed product description which lists benefits and ingredients clearly. 
The site also states that its products are “ proudly bottled in South Africa by Maskscara ” , indicating a local supply chain. 
However, the website has no customer reviews which prevents new customers from gauging product quality or service reliability. 
The website also lacks the “about us” page which indicate lack of transparency.
The company should create a clear “about us” page that includes a physical address and a working phone number. 
They should actively collect and display customer reviews  
## proposed website features and functionality
Homepage – will include the company logo and well as the navigation bar 
The about us page – highlights the company background, mission and vision statement as well as the target audience. 
Contact page – will include the company email, contact details and review space. 
Services page – highlights the services provided as well as the cost of each service. 
Desired functionality -  the website should welcome the user and guide them through the booking process. 
## design and user experience
The website will make use of bright colours such as neon pink to attract and retain users attention. 
The font arquitecta will be used to add character to the  website. 
The navigation bar will be at the top where it is going to be visible for the user.  
Homepage: About us,  Kat Claws and the business logo as well as the navigation bar, it will also have instructions to guide the user on what to do in order to book their appointments. 
## technical requirements
HTML will be used for the structure of the website, such as the headings, services lists. 
CSS will be for the style, adding more character to the website such as colours, responsive layout. 
JavaScript will be used to bring the website to live by using interactive elements. 
## timeline and milestones 
Week 1 of the project will be dedicated to research and gathering of information of the desired company. 
Week 2 is for planning the propasal as well as drafting ideas on our weebsite. 
Week 3 is creating the structure of our website using HTML.
